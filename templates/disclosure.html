Affiliate disclosure

Some outbound links on this site are affiliate links. If you subscribe to a tool after following one, the vendor may pay us a commission. You pay exactly the same price either way. Links that earn us a commission are labelled affiliate link at the point you click them, and every page carries the disclosure banner at the top.

What commission does not buy: inclusion, exclusion, ordering, a better price, or a softer entry in the change log. The figures are produced by a script that has no knowledge of which vendors pay us. That is not a promise about our character; it is a property of how the code is written, and the method is published so you can judge it.

Not advice

Nothing here is purchasing, financial, or legal advice. Prices change without notice, regional pricing differs, and we can be wrong. Always confirm the current price on the vendor's own site before you commit to anything. Every figure is published with the date it was last verified precisely so you can judge how much to trust it.

Privacy

This site is static HTML. It sets no cookies, runs no JavaScript, embeds no analytics, no advertising tags, no fonts or images from third parties, and no tracking pixels. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data because we have no mechanism to.

Two things do happen outside our control. Our host serves the pages and may keep standard server logs. And if you click an outbound link, the destination site — including any affiliate network in between — will see that visit and may set its own cookies under its own privacy policy. Outbound links carry rel="nofollow sponsored noopener" and a strict-origin-when-cross-origin referrer policy, so the page you came from is not passed along in full.
